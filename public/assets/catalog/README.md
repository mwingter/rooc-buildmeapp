# Catalog assets

No game artwork or copied assets are included in this repository.

Icons and illustrations in this catalog must have explicit permission or a compatible license.
Record the source and license for each asset in the `CatalogItem.license` field or in asset metadata.

Placeholder assets may be neutral CSS/text representations only.

Skill-tree icons must not be copied from Ragnarok Origin, its clients or unofficial mirrors.
Only original icons or assets explicitly licensed by Gravity may replace the neutral
initials shown by `SkillTreeEditor`.
